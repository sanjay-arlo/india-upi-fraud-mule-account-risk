# BA Handoff — India UPI Fraud & Mule-Account Risk

## Objective
Prioritise fraud-review workload using transaction anomalies, account behaviour, reporting speed and mule-account signals.

## Stakeholders
Fraud operations; risk analytics; compliance; banking operations; customer protection teams; model-risk/governance; audit.

## Core requirements
- Rank cases by transparent risk score.
- Quantify loss and recovery exposure.
- Track recovery-window compliance.
- Explain contributing signals.
- Prevent risk scores from being treated as proof of fraud.

## Decision rule
Prioritise cases with stronger anomaly combinations and higher loss exposure, while retaining authorised human review.

## Acceptance criteria
Risk and recovery KPIs reconcile; filters work; ranked cases expose drivers; privacy/synthetic-data disclaimers remain visible.

## Production controls
Confirmed fraud outcomes, device/account/network features, model validation, explainability, access controls, audit logs, privacy-by-design and feedback loops.
